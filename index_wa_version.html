The 5-Stage Intraday Blueprint Flow

WHY LEARN A STRUCTURED APPROACH?

Understanding a trading strategy involves more than knowing when to enter a trade. It requires understanding the complete process from end to end:

STAGE 01

STOCK SELECTION

Filter the market for liquid momentum candidates with elevated relative volume.

STAGE 02

PRICE ACTION

Study candlestick structure, support/resistance zones, and multi-timeframe trends.

STAGE 03

STRATEGY SETUP

Validate pre-trade criteria before considering an order. No confirmation, no trade.

STAGE 04

TRADE PLANNING

Determine entry trigger, exact invalidation level (stop loss), and profit targets.

STAGE 05

POSITION SIZING

Calculate exact share quantity based on capital risk tolerance before pressing execute.

STOCK SELECTION → PRICE ACTION → STRATEGY SETUP → TRADE PLANNING → POSITION SIZING

This workshop brings these concepts together in one structured educational experience so you never enter a trade without a predefined reason and plan.

The Science & Reality of Trading

Why Random Entries & Guesswork Fail in Intraday

Trading is not about predicting the future — it is an exercise in probability, discipline, and mathematical risk management.

⚖️

Predefined Math

Even a 50% win-rate strategy generates positive expectancy if reward outweighs risk and losses are strictly capped.

🧠

Emotional Detachment

When your trade setup has clear rules and your risk is predetermined in rupees, panic and anxiety disappear.

📐

Dynamic Sizing

Fixed share quantities cause catastrophic drawdowns on volatile stocks. Position sizing must adapt to the chart.

❌ Conventional Guesswork & Tips
Random Decisions

Entering based on news headlines, chasing green candles, moving stop-losses further away, and hoping for reversals.

✔ The Structured Blueprint Approach
Rule-Based Execution

Screening momentum stocks with documented parameters, confirming price action, and calculating exact position size before entering.
